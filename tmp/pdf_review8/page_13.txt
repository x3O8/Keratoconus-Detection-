8 Clinical Decision-Support Dashboard
The research prototype is delivered as a browser-based clinical decision-support
dashboard served by a FastAPI backend and a React frontend. Representative
interface views are shown in Fig. 3. The dashboard supports image-only, clinical-
only, and combined assessments, allowing an analysis to proceed when only one
modality is available.
Fig.3.Representative views of the KeratoScan research dashboard. The interface supports entry of up
to seven corneal-map images and one clinical record, displays branch-specific keratoconus probabilities
and their weighted late-fusion result, and provides image, clinical-feature, and evidence-retrieval expla-
nations.
Note:The dashboard is intended for research and decision-support use only. Its
outputs do not establish a diagnosis, treatment decision, disease stage, progres-
sion risk, or causal explanation, and require clinical review by an ophthalmolo-
gist.
Step 1 – Enter Available Data.The user may upload one or more corneal-
map images and/or a one-row CSV file containing the required clinical variables.
The image and clinical branches can run independently. When both are supplied,
the user can select the image-branch weight used in late probability fusion.
Step 2 – Review Model Outputs.The dashboard reports the keratoconus
probability and screen-positive/screen-negative result for each available branch.
