7 Experimental Results
Experimental Setup.All models were trained and evaluated on two NVIDIA
T4 GPUs running in parallel (30.5GB combined usable memory), 64GB RAM,
and 300GB of disk space, using PyTorch 2.0.1 and CUDA 11.8. Reported results
are the mean±standard deviation over three random seeds.
7.1 DS-1: Multi-Class Topography Classification
All four image encoders were evaluated on the held-out four-map test set
(n= 279). EfficientNetV2-S achieved the highest accuracy (98.92%), F1-score
(98.52%), and sensitivity (98.04%), while maintaining a test AUROC of 0.9989
and specificity of 99.44%. RETFound achieved the highest test AUROC (0.9993)
and specificity (100.00%). EfficientNetV2-S was selected as the preferred screen-
ing model because it provided the best balance between overall accuracy and
sensitivity, thereby minimizing missed Keratoconus cases. Full comparative
results appear in Table 4.
