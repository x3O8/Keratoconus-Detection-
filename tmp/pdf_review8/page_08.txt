5.2 Datasets
Three complementary datasets support training, validation, and benchmarking,
and are referred to throughout this paper by the shorthand DS-1, DS-2, and
DS-3 introduced in Section 2.
Dataset 1 (DS-1) – Corneal Topography Image Dataset (Multi-Class).
A multi-class ophthalmic image dataset with three classes: Normal, Keratoconus,
and Suspect KC, consisting of colour-coded corneal diagnostic maps in JPG
format from topography and tomography systems. The independent test set
contains 150 images (50 per class); the training and validation split contains 423
images (KC: 150, Normal: 150, Suspect: 123).
Dataset 2 (DS-2) – Structured Clinical / Keratoconus Topography
Dataset.A structured ophthalmologic dataset containing numerical measure-
ments from both left (OS) and right (OD) eyes: corneal curvature (Ks, Kf,
AvgK, CYL), pachymetry (Thinnest, Thickest, CSI_T), elevation indices (Max-
Elv, MinElv, RangeElv), symmetry and asymmetry metrics (DSI, OSI, SAI,
KCI), wavefront aberrations (coma, HOAs, SA.C40), and geometric features
(LocationX/Y, BFS_R).
Dataset 3 (DS-3) – TFRecord Binary Corneal Image Dataset.A bi-
nary Normal-vs-KC classification dataset stored in TensorFlow TFRecord for-
mat, organised as 18 training shards, 2 evaluation shards, and 1 test shard. This
benchmark suite was implemented in TensorFlow 2.x specifically, for its native
TFRecord tooling and to cross-validate baseline CNN results against a second,
independent framework; the proposed multimodal system itself (Sections 3–4) is
implemented entirely in PyTorch. Five state-of-the-art CNN architectures were
benchmarked on this dataset.
Dataset 4 (DS-4)CornOrb: A Multimodal Dataset of Orbscan Corneal Topog-
raphy This dataset includes 1,454 eyes out of 744 patients (889 normal and 565
keratoconus) with Orbscan 3 in Algerian ophthalmology centres. Four corneal
maps are provided for each eye (Axial, Anterior, Posterior, Pachymetry) and
clinical annotations (age, sex, astigmatism, K max, pachymetry, asphericity, di-
agnostic label) [11].
Note on longitudinal data.All three datasets above are cross-sectional, single-
visit collections; none contain repeat scans of the same eye over time. Head 2’s
severity/progression-risk score (Section 3.2) is therefore trained and validated on
single-visit data, and its extension to a true temporal rate-of-change predictor
awaits a longitudinal cohort, as discussed in Section??.
5.3 Preprocessing Pipeline
The preprocessing pipeline comprises: (1) artefact removal and quality screen-
ing for motion blur, decentration, and exposure artefacts; (2) topographic map
normalisation to zero mean and unit variance, resized to224×224; (3) Z-score
standardisation of structured biomechanical indices with imputation of miss-
ing values; (4) augmentation via random flip, rotation (±10◦), brightness jit-
ter (±0.1), and Gaussian noise; (5) Synthetic Minority Oversampling Technique
(SMOTE)oversamplingoftheunderrepresentedSuspectclassinDS-1,appliedto
EfficientNet-B0 embedding vectors rather than raw pixels so that no unrealistic
synthetic images are introduced, together with zero-filling for missing modalities;
and (6) TFRecord sharding for DS-3 with paralleltf.dataprefetch pipelines to
keep GPU utilisation high during training.
