4.3 Explainability and Reasoning Layer
Explainability Layer:Integrated Gradients[26] attribute the prediction to in-
put features by integrating gradients along the path from a baseline to the actual
input, giving theoretically grounded, additive attributions;Attention Rollout[1]
propagates attention weights across layers to identify the image regions globally
relevant to each class decision; andGradCAM[23] heatmaps highlight the patho-
logical corneal regions – inferior steepening, posterior elevation – contributing
most to a given prediction.
RAG System:An embedding-based retriever, orchestrated with LangChain
over a FAISS similarity index [9], pulls domain-specific clinical passages relevant
to the predicted stage and the model’s own XAI attributions. The retrieved
evidence, the stage prediction, and the attribution scores are then passed as
structured context to a Gemini LLM [28], which synthesises them into a con-
cise four-to-five sentence clinical narrative – explicitly referencing the retrieved
evidence and the flagged regions rather than generating a free-form summary.
4.4 Output Layer
Thesystemdeliverssixclinicallyactionableoutputs,drawnfromthethreepredic-
tion heads and the reasoning layer described above: (1)KC Stage– the graded
classification from Head 1; (2)Severity & Progression-Risk Estimate– a
continuous score from Head 2 indicating current disease severity and urgency for
follow-up; (3)Asymmetry Flag– the binary inter-ocular signal from Head 3;
(4)Explanation– a clinically grounded LLM narrative; (5)Modality Impor-
tance– per-modality attention weights quantifying relative contribution; and
(6)Feature Importance– tabular biomarker rankings.
5 Methodology
5.1 Development Approach
Development followed an iterative Agile lifecycle across six phases: (1) data col-
lection and preprocessing, (2) exploratory data analysis and feature engineering,
(3) model design and fine-tuning, (4) RAG integration, (5) evaluation and com-
parative benchmarking, and (6) Streamlit dashboard deployment, with feature-
importance results from Phase 5 looping back to prune noisy biomechanical
indices before retraining the tabular model.
