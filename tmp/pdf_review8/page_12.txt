7.2 DS-2: Structured Clinical Feature Classification
Five tabular models were evaluated on the locked test set (n= 277). Random
Forest achieved the highest test AUROC (0.9996), whereas XGBoost achieved
the best threshold-based screening performance, with 98.19% accuracy, 95.10%
sensitivity, a 97.49% F1-score, and the lowest Brier score (0.0136). LightGBM
attained the same confusion-matrix performance as XGBoost but had a slightly
lower test AUROC and a higher Brier score. All models produced zero false pos-
itives on this test set, corresponding to 100% specificity. Accordingly, XGBoost
was selected as the preferred structured clinical and topographic screening model.

7.3 DS-3: Binary TFRecord CNN Benchmarking
InceptionV3 was the strongest binary classifier, reaching 86.10% accuracy, an
F1-score of 0.8561, and an AUC of 0.9465. MobileNetV2 followed closely with
82.36% accuracy and an AUC of 0.9014. ResNet50 and EfficientNetB0 struggled
with unstable convergence and poor discrimination on the minority class in this
binary setting, which further motivates the proposed multimodal fusion rather
than relying on any single CNN backbone. Full results appear in Table 6.
