Step 3 – Inspect Model Explanations.For the image branch, the inter-
face presents Grad-CAM overlays, Integrated Gradients maps, and a single-layer
residual attention rollout across the supplied corneal-map modalities. For the
clinical branch, TreeSHAP contributions rank the patient-specific clinical vari-
ables that increased or decreased the model output. These explanations describe
model behaviour and should not be interpreted as causal or diagnostic evidence.
Step 4 – Retrieve Supporting Evidence.The dashboard retrieves relevant

9 Conclusion and Future Work
This work presents KeratoScan, a multimodal, explainable research decision-
supportworkflowforbinarykeratoconusscreening.Thesystemcombinesaseven-
map EfficientNet-B0 image branch with four-head modality attention and a sep-
arately trained, calibrated XGBoost clinical branch. Each branch can be used
independently; when both inputs are available, their probabilities are combined
through configurable late fusion. The interface also provides patient-specific
Grad-CAM, Integrated Gradients, modality-attention, and TreeSHAP explana-
tions, together with retrieval of relevant clinical evidence.
The results show strong branch-level performance on their respective held-
out test data. The seven-map EfficientNet-B0 image model achieved an accuracy
of 94.00%, AUROC of 0.9904, and macro-F1 score of 0.9290 on the binary DS-
