5.4 Training Configuration
Table 3 lists the hyperparameters used to train the image branch; the tabular
XGBoost branch uses the 100-estimator configuration noted in the final row. As
a lightweight reference point against the pre-trained EfficientNet-B0 and Con-
vNeXt backbones, the “CNN” baseline in Section 7 is a compact four-block con-
volutional network (32-64-128-256 filters,3×3kernels, batch normalisation, and
max-pooling, followed by a 256-unit dense head) trained from scratch under the
same schedule.
